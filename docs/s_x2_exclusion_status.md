## 4. Exact blockers

1. `s_x2_r32_valuation_lemma.md` proves
   `v_p(D_m)=v_p(A-B)+v_p(m-c_p)` for primes with
   `p | A-B` and `p | D_m`. This is exact but is a consequence of the
   no-primitive-factor assumption and does not by itself close
   S-R3.2. No direct generalized primitive-divisor theorem has been
   found for `D_m=X A^m-Y B^m`.
2. `verify_s_x2_p3_h3_counterexample.py` gives exact MOD3+H3 and
   MOD9+H3 word shapes for Q=17, P=371253907. Thus the p=3 gate
   cannot close that record even with H3.
3. For several tested primitive primes
   `p in {7, 89, 10337, 2879, 9209}` on the Q=17 records, the
   A-family U=0 all-ones rise word admits a C3 excess sequence with
   `A_tot == 0 mod p`. MODp alone cannot close those records; H_exact
   is still required.
4. A conservative source-class induction for L-B' using the least
   positive representative of each residue class fails at depth 8-9:
   classes modulo `5^8` and `5^9` with residues 5599 and 11198 have
   required `theta` about 10.5-11.3 and non-empty danger sets. This
   confirms that L-B'a/b needs a real lower bound on the word-level
   minimal starts, not just the residue representative.

## 4bis. Literature audit for S-R3.2

`s_x2_r32_granville_audit.md` records the result of checking the
standard primitive-divisor literature. Granville's theorem covers
standard Lucas sequences x_n=(r^n-s^n)/(r-s) with x_0=0,x_1=1; it
does not directly cover D_m=X A^m-Y B^m. No direct theorem for the
generalized initial-value sequence was found. S-R3.2 therefore remains
an open adaptation, and the small-prime certificate remains the only
certified coverage.

## 4ter. L-B' reduction progress

`s_x3_3_lb_ub2_reduction.md` proves that L-B' reduces to words with
U <= 2, that U=0 is closed by exact formulas, and that U=1 is closed
by two explicit lower bounds. The only remaining open subcase of
L-B'a/b is U=2. The exact U=2 equation is written in Section 7 of
that document; a 2,573,499-word certificate for L<=250 has fails=0,
but the analytic U=2 bound is not yet complete.

continued-fraction machinery of verify_surv_ray.py. That machinery
truncates at denominators around 10^80, so the reported counts and the
apparent zero counts for Q>=211 were artifacts. The low-precision
file has been deleted.

The figure "15,377 records for Q=101..200" is not a valid
certificate: it came from the truncated 160-digit scan and has been

- Q=211..215: 18 records (`surv_ray_extension_q211_215_prec220.txt`)
- Q=216..225: 18 records (`surv_ray_extension_q216_225_prec220.txt`)

S-X3.3c route via L-B'/U=2 bypasses the finite enumeration, so proving
Q > 100 empty is unnecessary (2026-08-12).
