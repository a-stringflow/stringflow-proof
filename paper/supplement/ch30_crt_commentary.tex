\section{The CRT layer: statement-by-statement commentary}

This chapter comments on each statement in the CRT layer of
\texttt{UnifiedCoreAudit.lean}.  The statements are recorded together
with their mathematical role and their current status.

\subsection{\texttt{All36\_20PremisesNoHge}}

This is the record of the no-$H_{\ge}$ premises.  It carries the
block-head data
\begin{equation*}
  j,\ W_p,\ W_j,\ q,\ A_j,\ A_s,\ s,\ W_s,\ r_s,\ L,\ H_s
\end{equation*}
and the step-weight function $\mathrm{weight}$.  The record is a
definition, not a theorem; it is compiled.

\subsection{\texttt{blockB\_\allowbreak bound\_\allowbreak of\_\allowbreak
no\_\allowbreak hge}}

This statement derives the block-tail bound
\begin{equation*}
  3B+2^{\Delta}\le3\cdot5^n-2\cdot4^n,
\end{equation*}
where $n=s-j$ and $\Delta=W_s-W_j$.  It is the first size condition
used by the CRT argument.  The statement is compiled.

\subsection{\texttt{rj0\_\allowbreak spec\_\allowbreak eq}}

This statement packages the CRT equation
\begin{equation*}
  3\cdot5^n\,r_{j,0}+3B+2^{\Delta}
    =2^{\Delta+L+4}\left(u+m'\,2^{H_s-1}\right)
\end{equation*}
and the definition of the candidate $r_{j,0}$ as its least
nonnegative solution.  The statement is compiled.

\subsection{\texttt{terminal\_\allowbreak bound\_\allowbreak iff\_\allowbreak
yStar\_\allowbreak ne\_\allowbreak zero}}

This statement records the equivalence
\begin{equation*}
  \vtwo{5^{L+3}w_{\mathrm{term}}+1}\le H_s-2
  \iff y^*\ne0.
\end{equation*}
The residue $y^*$ is defined by
\begin{equation*}
  y^*=
  -\left(w_{\mathrm{term}}+5^{-(L+3)}\right)
  \left(3\cdot5^s\right)^{-1}
  \pmod{2^{H_s-1}}.
\end{equation*}
The statement is compiled.

\subsection{\texttt{rj0\_\allowbreak ge\_\allowbreak of\_\allowbreak
size\_\allowbreak conditions\_\allowbreak no\_\allowbreak hge}}

This statement derives
\begin{equation*}
  r_{j,0}\ge5^j
\end{equation*}
from the two size conditions.  The derivation is the inequality chain
recorded in the main paper.  The statement is compiled.

\subsection{\texttt{unified\_\allowbreak core\_\allowbreak final\_\allowbreak
no\_\allowbreak hge}}

This is the final valuation inequality
\begin{equation*}
  \vtwo{5^{L+3}w_{\mathrm{term}}+1}\le H_s-2
\end{equation*}
under the no-$H_{\ge}$ premises, the block-head equality, the
full-orbit reachability of $r$, and $2\le H_s$.  The statement is
the unique \texttt{sorry} in the unified-core audit and is pending.
